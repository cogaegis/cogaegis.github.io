How accurate is it?

No detector is proof. We show a likelihood, the evidence behind it, and stay in the middle of the scale when signals disagree.

What happens to my files?

Uploads are deleted within 24 hours unless you save the report.

Do failed scans count?

No. If we can't analyze something, it doesn't use up a scan.
