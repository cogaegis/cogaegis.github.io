Beginner AI Literacy Program

Your first steps into the world of AI. A friendly, hands-on program for people who are new to AI, including seniors and anyone who finds new technology a challenge. Learn what AI is, how to talk with it, and how to tell what's real.

What You'll Learn

Three 50-minute sessions, each building on the last, with plenty of time to practise on your own phone, tablet or laptop.

Beginner 101

Discover

50 minutes · Free

Understand what AI is and set up your own account.

  • What AI and generative AI are, in plain language
  • 5 everyday uses: writing, cooking, planning, creating and learning
  • Meet ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude, and which to start with
  • Create your own ChatGPT or Gemini account, step by step, with a helper beside you
Beginner 102

Communicate

50 minutes

Talk to AI as easily as texting a friend.

  • Start a conversation: no special commands, and typos are fine
  • Write useful prompts with the 5 W's (who, what, where, when, why)
  • AI chat vs. a search engine: a side-by-side test
  • Create something with AI, then Prompt → Review → Refine
Beginner 103

Recognize

50 minutes

Stay safe, and keep your own judgment.

  • Why AI can be confidently wrong, invent sources or simply flatter you
  • What research says about over-reliance and critical thinking
  • When to use AI, and when not to
  • Spot AI-generated videos, voices and writing

By the end of the program, you'll be able to:

  • Set up and use ChatGPT or Google Gemini on your own device
  • Ask clear questions and get genuinely useful answers
  • Use AI for everyday tasks, light research and creative projects
  • Double-check AI answers before trusting them
  • Recognize AI-generated content and scams
  • Use AI without giving up your own thinking and creativity
Program Delivery Style

How We Teach

A look inside the Beginner program, with real slides from our sessions. Select any slide to view it full size.

1

Easy to follow, with examples you can quickly relate to

Every session uses customized, personalized examples, like Carol, a Langford grandmother who uses AI to find a birthday gift, pick a nearby store and plan her bus trip.

2

Hands-on experience with plenty of on-site helpers

You practise on your own device from the very first session, with about one helper for every eight learners. Mistakes are okay: you can't break anything by asking a question.

3

An introduction to real-world risks, and how to handle them

Learn why AI can be confidently wrong, and how to spot AI-generated images, video and audio, so you stay informed, not fooled.

Program Details

Who it's for

  • People with little or no knowledge of AI
  • Anyone who struggles with new technology but is willing to learn
  • People who are vulnerable to AI-generated fraud and scams
  • Anyone who needs a basic level of AI literacy

What to bring

Please bring one device: a smartphone, tablet or laptop. You'll also need one of the following to sign in to AI tools:

  • An email address (Gmail preferred)
  • A Facebook account
  • A mobile phone number

Host a session

Libraries, seniors' centres and community groups can host this program for their members. A suitable venue has:

  • Seats for everyone, washrooms, electricity and Wi-Fi
  • Ideally a large screen (HDMI or wireless), tables, and heating or cooling

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any experience with AI or computers?

No. The Beginner program is designed for people with little or no experience. If you can use a smartphone, tablet or laptop, you can take part, and there is about one helper for every eight learners.

Which AI tools will I learn to use?

We introduce ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude, and help you create your own ChatGPT or Gemini account in Beginner 101. Everything in Beginner 102 works the same way in either one.

Is this suitable for seniors?

Yes. Many participants are seniors or people who find new technology challenging. Sessions go at a comfortable pace with everyday examples, and Beginner 103 focuses on spotting AI-generated content and scams.

How much does it cost?

For individuals, Beginner 101 is free and the Beginner 102 + 103 workshop is $69 per person. For organizations, the full three-session series is $100 per learner, with a $750 minimum booking and $75 per learner beyond 25.

Can my library, seniors' centre or community group host a session?

Yes. The program is designed to run at community venues with seats, washrooms, electricity and Wi-Fi. New organization partners can book Beginner 101 as a free demo.

Can I register a parent or grandparent?

Yes. You can register on someone else's behalf. Just contact us with their name and the session you'd like them to attend.
